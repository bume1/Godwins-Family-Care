Community Care Liaison
Pay Sheet & How You Earn

One role, four ways to earn. You own relationships over their life, and you're paid on how long care lasts, not just when it starts.
Final · Community Care Liaison · Remote · August 2026
1

The Role, In One Paragraph

W2 · PRN · Part-time

You are a W2 employee of Godwins Family Care, working part-time (PRN) and fully remote from Texas into our Georgia market, on a schedule that grows with your results. This replaces the old setter/closer split: a liaison opens facility doors by phone and video, closes families, and stays close to them, because your pay continues for as long as your clients stay on service. GFC's local team runs every on-site step (demos, assessments, starts of care); you create and warm the relationships that get them in the room. Being a W2 employee is not paperwork trivia. It is what legally allows you to market our clinical services to every patient in the building, including Medicare, Medicaid, and commercially insured, which no 1099 rep can do.

The mindset shift: a sales rep is paid when someone signs. A liaison is paid while someone stays. Close families that fit, keep them happy, and your book pays you every month.

Average vs core

The true private-pay home care average runs $16–18k per year per client, about 10–12 hours of care a week, staying roughly a year. That's your bread-and-butter close. The 20-hour "core" client is the one you grow them into.

Four Ways You're Paid

Acquire · Retain · Own
Stream 1 · Outreach Hours

Guaranteed hourly, every week

$18 / hour · 6 to 12 hrs per week

Paid through payroll for scheduled, documented outreach: calls and video meetings with facility decision-makers, virtual family consults, booking on-site demos for the GFC team, CRM time. You work 6 to 12 hours a week. You submit your availability two weeks in advance and GFC schedules your hours around it, so this fits alongside your full-time role.

Stream 2 · Home Care Commission

Three phases, one client

12% → 7% → 3% acquisition, retention, ownership

Acquisition, Month 1: 12% of collected billing, never less than $200. Retention, Months 2–12: 7%, never less than $90/month. Account ownership, Year 2: 3%. At the end of year two the client becomes a house account. Your % rides the client's entire private-pay invoice, meaning care hours plus add-ons like Continuous Care monitoring ($299/mo) and LMSW case management. Upsells raise your check automatically.

Stream 3 · Concierge (DPC) Commission

Small dues, long life

50% → 10% → 5% same three phases

50% of Month 1 membership dues, 10% of Months 2–12, 5% in Year 2, then house account. On a $200/month membership: $100, then $20/month. These clients stay for years and stack quietly underneath your home care book.

Stream 4 · Community Partner Ladder

Build an account, get paid three times

$900–950 / fully developed community

$150 when a community comes on as a GFC partner and its first client is on service. Both conditions are required: a partnership on its own does not pay. $250 to $300 when that community has produced 5 clients on service, set by GFC at the milestone based on the value of the book that community produced. $500 at 10 clients. Cumulative and lifetime per account, counting distinct clients who actually started care. One well-worked assisted living community can pay the ladder in full and keep referring underneath your 12/7/3.

Account Ownership · The Activity Gate

Keep what you work

Open a facility account and every commission-eligible client it refers is yours for as long as you're actually working it. An account stays yours while it meets two tests: (1) it produced at least one eligible referral in the last quarter, and (2) your touches are documented in the CRM. Fail both for a quarter and it converts to a house account. The gate governs the account: keep it alive and every new client it refers starts a fresh 12/7/3 under your name. Each individual client runs its own two-year course and then becomes a house account.

Why this beats the old rule: the previous structure took accounts away at 6 months no matter what. This one never takes away an account you're working, and never pays anyone for an account they've abandoned.

The Growth Path

PRN today · Full-time trigger in writing

This role is built to convert. When your sourced book holds $25,000/month of recurring revenue for 2 consecutive months, the role converts to a full-time salaried Community Care Liaison position with base salary plus this commission structure, negotiated at conversion. That trigger lives in your offer letter, not in a promise. You're not taking a part-time job; you're building your own full-time one.

Terms in plain language. The Community Care Liaison is a part-time (PRN) W2 employee of Godwins Family Care, working remotely from Texas into GFC's Georgia market. Outreach hours: $18/hour for pre-approved, documented outreach activity, starting at 6 hours/week; each client placed on service adds 2 approved hours/week to a cap of 12; hours are scheduled and may be adjusted weekly by GFC. Home care commission, in three phases: acquisition, 12% of Month-1 collected billing ($200 minimum); retention, 7% of Months 2–12 ($90/month minimum); account ownership, 3% of Year 2, after which the client becomes a house account. Applies to the client's full private-pay invoice including eligible add-ons. Concierge commission: 50% / 10% / 5% of collected membership dues on the same schedule. All commissions and floors apply to collected revenue only, are paid after the client completes each service month, prorate if a client pauses or leaves mid-month, and are payable only while employed. Community partner ladder, cumulative and lifetime per account: $150 when a community has both come on as a GFC referral partner and produced its first client on service (both conditions required; a partnership alone does not pay), $250 to $300 once that community has produced 5 distinct clients on service as set by GFC at the milestone, $500 at 10. Account attribution follows the activity gate in Section 8. Medicare, Medicaid, and commercially insured patients are never commissioned per-patient under any circumstance; marketing of those services is compensated through hourly wages and pre-set quarterly program bonuses in accordance with the federal bona fide employee provision of the Anti-Kickback Statute. Commission rates are guaranteed for the first 6 months of employment and reviewed thereafter; rate changes apply to new clients only, and clients already on your book keep their original rates. New home care clients are billed two weeks in advance with a two-week deposit at start of care.
