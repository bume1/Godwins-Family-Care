Email Copy Library.

Every automated email the sequence engine sends, exactly as the recipient reads it. The first email changes by facility type and by who the contact is. Follow-ups change by facility type. Know what a facility already received before you walk in.

Changing the copy

All of this lives in the Email Templates database in Notion. Change a subject line or a value prop there and this page updates within five minutes. Nothing to republish.

A row set to Draft is skipped by the engine, which is how an email is taken out of rotation without breaking anything.
