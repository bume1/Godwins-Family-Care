The Daily Loop

Every working session
  1. Open the targeting map and pick your name. Every call you log stamps your name to the record. That stamp is your attribution evidence; without it, your accounts and your commissions have no trail.
  2. Work your Tasks panel first, then new leads. Overdue and today's follow-ups before fresh dials. Filter the map to uncontacted facilities when prospecting.
  3. Call using the script for that vertical. The Cold Call Script doc carries the talk track, decision-maker path, qualifying questions, and objection handling for every facility type. Fast lane: PCH and SNF discharge desks. Trust lane: ALF and ILF. The win condition is the booked meeting, demo, or lunch-and-learn.
  4. Log every touch the same day. Result, notes, meeting date, follow-up. The server confirms each save. Your notes also feed the email engine's personalization, so the better the note, the better every automated touch that follows.
  5. Confirmed interest? Send to Notion. Use the row's one-time Send to Notion button: pick the pipeline stage, keep the default follow-up task (3 business days) unless there's a reason not to. That creates the linked Partner and Contact records. From then on your map edits sync to Notion automatically and the contact enters the sequence engine.
  6. Book the meeting; loop in Dr. Ume. You earn the meeting or demo; Dr. Ume coordinates everything on-site in Georgia. Prep tailored materials and join virtually where possible.
  7. Family consult → book the assessment. Use your own booking page for video consults. You answer questions and schedule the in-home assessment; a GFC clinician performs it and closes the plan of care. Always.
  8. Stay close after the start of care. Regular account reviews, quick resolution of service concerns with Dr. Ume, and growing referral volume from accounts that already know you. This is the part of the job that builds your residual income.
  9. Close the loop before you log off. Tasks updated, hours logged in Rippling, tomorrow's follow-ups visible in the panel.

Non-Negotiables

Short list, zero exceptions
  • Same-day documentation. Your agreement makes contemporaneous CRM documentation a condition of variable pay, and accounts stay yours only while they show documented touches.
  • Hours are pre-approved and logged. Availability submitted two weeks ahead; GFC schedules within it; every hour lands in Rippling.
  • Approved materials, approved claims. Nothing beyond GFC's vetted collateral and scripts. It's always "Godwins Family Care" or "GFC," and physician and FNP-owned goes in the room every time.
  • HIPAA and confidentiality. Minimum necessary, GFC systems only, portal materials never forwarded. The Employee Handbook carries the full policies you've signed.

Your First Ninety Days

Portal → Pipeline → Your book
Days 1–30

Live in the Portal

The portal is the center of your sales activity this month: prospecting, working the targeting map, and documenting outreach. Dr. Ume walks every area of it with you in your kickoff training — collateral, demo decks, and workflows like scheduling demos and lunches — then coaches you through each lead and facility type live, including the scripting, so you can make initial contact and book meetings with confidence. Your job this month is motion: dials, logged touches, and meetings on the calendar. When interest or need is mutually confirmed, you move the lead from the portal database into Notion with the handoff button.

Done looks like

  • All five systems live and tested, handbook and acknowledgments signed, availability submitted
  • Portal training complete; every script, doc, and map feature familiar enough to use without looking
  • Consistent same-day logging on every touch, from day one
  • First meetings booked with facility decision-makers, and first qualified leads handed off to Notion
  • Comfortable explaining home health vs home care vs house calls in thirty seconds
Days 31–60

Run the Pipeline

Once you're fluid in the portal, Dr. Ume trains you on the Notion revenue operations side: the full sales pipeline and the automations that nurture your deals through to closed, live patients and facility clients. This month you manage handed-off contacts through their stages, let the sequence engine carry the email cadence while you carry the relationships, and start converting meetings into demos, family consults, and booked assessments delivered by the Georgia team.

Done looks like

  • Notion pipeline training complete; you manage your own stages, tasks, and follow-ups without prompting
  • Demos and lunch-and-learns booked and delivered with the GFC local team
  • First family consults run on your own booking page, with assessments scheduled for clinicians
  • First client on service, or visibly close — the pay sheet's model pace is two average clients a month
Days 61–90

Own the Book

By now the loop is yours end to end: you source from the leads GFC feeds you, work them through the pipeline, and stay close to every account after the start of care. This month the focus shifts from learning to owning — regular account reviews, resolving service concerns with Dr. Ume, and growing referral volume from accounts that already know you. Residual commission is the point of this role; the book you tend this month is the one that pays you monthly from here forward.

Done looks like

  • Pipeline fully self-managed: prospecting cadence, stages, tasks, and logging run without oversight
  • Clients on service and paying accounts under your name, pacing toward two starts a month
  • Every active account healthy under the activity gate — a documented touch and referral momentum each quarter
  • A standing check-in cadence with Dr. Ume and clean coordination on every on-site step you book
  • You can walk into day 91 and describe your book, your pipeline, and your next five moves without opening a screen
