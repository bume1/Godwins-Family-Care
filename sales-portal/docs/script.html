Handling Objections

Stay Ready
"We don't allow outside agencies."
Understood. Is that policy across the board, or are there exceptions for residents who need more support than your staffing levels allow? Our scope often complements yours rather than duplicating it. Continuous skilled nursing is something most facilities aren't staffed to provide. We follow your protocols and stay invisible to your team.
"Families handle their own caregiver arrangements."
That makes sense. Do you have a resource list you provide to families when they ask? We'd love to be included as a vetted, clinician-owned option. Worth noting too: most families don't realize private duty skilled nursing is even available outside home health. Having that option ready to recommend tends to help them.
"We're fully staffed and don't need overflow help."
Glad to hear it. That probably won't always be the case, holidays, call-outs, unexpected situations. We'd love to be the backup resource when those moments arise. Also worth flagging: we specialize in behavioral and dementia 1:1, plus continuous skilled nursing under our state license. Different lane from typical agencies.
"We already have preferred agencies."
Most preferred lists are either personal care companies or home health agencies. We span both lanes under one state license, which is unusual. Worth having for the cases that fall in between, especially behavioral or skilled nursing 1:1 that current providers may not cover.
"How much do you charge?"
Personal care starts at $26/hour. Continuous skilled nursing is quoted by case based on acuity and shift length. Most families pay private, LTC insurance, VA, or Medicaid waiver (Katie Beckett, ICWP). We're working toward Medicaid certification.
