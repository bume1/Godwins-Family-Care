# Care plan templates, standard interventions and evidence-based pick-lists (owner request, 2026-10-14)

Owner ask: cut the documentation burden of the eight-section plan with smart defaults, pre-populated dropdowns (especially interventions and expected outcomes) and one-click templates for common pathways like CHF or fall risk; standardize intervention description and type as selectable lists for decision support and reporting; and use evidence-based suggestion lists from OpenEMR or another reputable source.

- **`carePlanCatalog.js` is the one catalog**, served on `GET …/care-plan` as `catalog`; the form restates none of it (build-enforced).
- **Standard interventions (45), each with a stable `code`, a fixed type, a default owner and frequency, and its Omaha System category.** The form offers them as a grouped dropdown with "Other (type your own)". A coded row's TYPE always comes from the catalog, whatever is posted, so reporting and decision support can count it; its wording may be adjusted. Anything else saves as `code: 'custom'`. Saved rows keep `code` and `omaha`.
- **Pathway templates, one click each:** heart failure, fall risk, type 2 diabetes, COPD, high blood pressure, dementia. A click ADDS the problem (prognosis and expected outcome), goals (measure and target), interventions linked to those goals, caregiver tasks and warning signs. It never overwrites, never adds a line twice, and skips sections the client's service line does not have. Everything stays editable until the author signs.
- **Evidence, named on the form ("Evidence behind these suggestions" and on each button):** AHA/ACC/HFSA 2022 heart failure guideline and the AHA self-check plan (call for a gain of more than 2-3 lb in a day or 5 lb in a week); CDC STEADI and USPSTF 2024 for falls (fall-risk medication review, orthostatic BP, PT for strength and balance, home safety, vision, feet and footwear); ADA Standards of Care, Older Adults (A1C goal individualized by health status, low blood sugar under 70 mg/dL); GOLD for COPD (inhaler technique, action plan, pulmonary rehab; oxygen target is the prescriber's); 2025 AHA/ACC blood pressure guideline (under 130/80, individualized for frailty); Alzheimer's Association practice recommendations and CMS GUIDE for dementia. Interventions are grouped by the Omaha System, the public-domain home health terminology (in the NLM UMLS as OMS). Targets and signs are starting points to individualize, and the form says so.
- **Why not OpenEMR:** this instance has ICD-10 loaded and no care plan code set (SNOMED CT is not loaded), and the app reads OpenEMR only inside a clinician's own session. No SNOMED, LOINC or Omaha target code is written into the catalog (build-enforced) until a loaded code set can check it.
- **Pick-lists:** prognosis and expected outcome offer standard phrases and still accept typing.
- **Smart defaults:** a new plan starts effective today with a 90-day review; choosing a review cadence fills the next review date. Pharmacy, hospital and outside providers already start from intake.
- **Verified:** `test/care_plan_catalog.test.js` (11 tests). Full suite 2486 pass, 0 fail. Rendered in Chromium for a clinical and a home care client: two template clicks filled 2 problems, 4 goals, 15 coded interventions linked to the right goals, prognosis, outcomes, warning signs and the review dates; on the home care client the same clicks added no prognosis or interventions; no page errors.
