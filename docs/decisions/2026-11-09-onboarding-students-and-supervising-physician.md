# Onboarding: students (Chapter H) and the supervising physician (Reference R1, Georgia)

**Date:** 2026-11-09 · **Source:** Onboarding Playbook v3.0, Chapter H and Reference R1

## Chapter H: student or extern
A student is on an educational placement, not employed. The phases are Affiliation Agreement, Clearance, and Orientation & Placement.
- **Affiliation Agreement:**
  - placement classified as educational (FLSA, *Statutory*, H.2), with uncertain cases going to the caregiver track
  - affiliation agreement, with the liability coverage position confirmed
  - Code of Conduct and HIPAA acknowledgments
  - the Field Addendum, only for a residence placement
- **Clearance:** background check, TB and immunizations, and BLS with its expiration tracked.
- **Orientation & Placement:**
  - orientation
  - supervision and billing limits: always supervised, never billed (False Claims Act, *Statutory*), documentation countersigned
  - the Clinical Rotation Curriculum for weeks 3–8
- **Build-enforced:** no Employee Handbook, no I-9/W-4/direct deposit, no offer or payroll item, and no recurring payroll duty.

## Reference R1: supervising (delegating) physician, Georgia only
- **Phase 1, Verify & Engage:**
  - physician verified (license, board certification, DEA, malpractice, NPDB, OIG, SAM, board records, comparable specialty)
  - the physician agreement with **fair market value compensation documented** (R1.1)
  - remaining capacity checked against Georgia's limit of **8** APRN/PA equivalents
  - a designated alternate physician
- **Phase 2, Agreements & Filing:**
  - one nurse protocol agreement per clinician, filed with the Composite Medical Board
  - the recurring obligations scheduled
  - retention and exit arrangements
- **Recurring (R1.3), each with its rule citation:**
  - quarterly: controlled-substance record review, and evaluation of controlled-substance patients
  - within 30 days: adverse-outcome review
  - annually: 10% record review, on-site observation, agreement review
  - within 10 working days: Board notice of termination
- **What is not built:** R1.4's North Carolina and Alabama summaries (unverified per the Playbook; owner: Georgia only). R1.3 has no classification column, so nothing is classified.
- **Default:** a provider whose prescriber credential is MD or DO defaults to this track.
