# Onboarding Chapter E: the hybrid clinician (W-2 plus a 1099 exam contract)

**Date:** 2026-11-08 · **Source:** Onboarding Playbook v3.0, Chapter E · **Owner rule:** same day

## The split
- **Chapter D is for TRUE contractors only.** These are clinicians who are 1099 and nothing else. D's guards stay in place for them: no Handbook, no I-9/W-4, and no training.
- **Chapter E is for a hybrid.** That's a clinician employed (W-2) for clinical care who is also contracted (1099) for exams. Owner rule: they clear **every** W-2 requirement.
  - The employed side is Chapter C, whole and unchanged: Handbook, I-9/W-4, training, competency, orientation and readiness modules.
  - The contractor side is small and exists for payment classification only.

## What E adds on top of C
One phase, "Exam Contract & Payment", placed after the employment paperwork:
- **E.2.01** Independent contractor agreement for the exam work (counsel reviews both agreements together).
- **E.2.02** Form W-9 and vendor payment setup, kept separate from payroll.
- **E.2.03** The two engagements kept separate (Playbook E.3):
  - exam work is offered and accepted, never scheduled
  - clinical hours go on the GFC clock, exam work is paid by case
  - separate payment runs
  - the malpractice policy that covers each engagement

Items built once (license, NPI, the single protocol agreement, DEA, exclusion screening) are C's own items. They are not repeated.

## Accounts
- Employment Type gains **HYBRID**. A hybrid provider defaults to Chapter E.
- A hybrid clocks in on the shared clock **for clinical hours only**. My GFC says so.
