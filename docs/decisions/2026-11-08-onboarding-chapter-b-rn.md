## What changed

- **`onboarding/chapterB.js`** is transcribed from Playbook v3.0 Chapter B: a registered nurse employed on the private home care line. It has four phases:
  1. Pre-Hire Screening
  2. Offer & Employment Paperwork
  3. Clearance & Qualification ("before client contact")
  4. Orientation & Responsibilities
- **Classifications** are copied from the B.2 table (the B.8 table for recurring duties).
  - Examples: RN licensure is Regulatory; the criminal records check is Statutory and regulatory; BLS and drug screening are Internal policy.
  - Items B.2 does not cover carry a note saying so.
- **Delegated duties are conditional:**
  - "Supervisory responsibilities" and the supervisory-visit recurring duty appear only when the Clinical Director delegates supervision.
  - "Direction of the forty-hour training program" appears only when the nurse directs it.
- **Private Home Care P&P v3.1** is acknowledged, from the canonical copy in Policies / Current (`11tYIh4b…`, same folder as the Administrative P&P). The Playbook's B.10 names 3.0, which is kept on the record. **The owner should confirm.**

- **RN accounts default to Chapter B.** The office can change the chapter in Setup.
- **My GFC Policies** now lists the PHC P&P for RNs and caregivers.
