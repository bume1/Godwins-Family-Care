# A care plan reaches the patient only once a licensed clinician has signed it (owner request, 2026-10-14)

Owner ask: "care plan cannot be seen by the patient unless signed by a licensed clinician (RN for PHC and LCSW / Provider for clinical care plan)."

- **The rule is "active", and active already meant exactly this.** A plan is active unless it is waiting on a co-signature (`providerCoSignStatus === 'pending'`). `clinicalRoles.evaluateCarePlanSignature` already decides who completes a plan: an RN, provider or LCSW on a home care plan; a provider or LCSW on a clinical patient's. An LMSW-written plan (and, once #192 merges, a coordinator's) waits. One pure check, `carePlanFormat.isPlanActive`, answers it for the patient side. A plan written before the co-signature step carries no status and was signed by a clinician, so it counts as active.
- **While an update waits, the patient keeps the last active version.** `patientVisiblePlan` returns the current plan if active, otherwise the newest earlier stored version that was (a stored version that was pending counts once its co-signature is on the row). A family is never left with no instructions because an update is in review. The portal says "An updated plan is with your care team for review" and offers no signature on the older version (`coSignable: false`).
- **No version ever active:** the portal shows no plan and says "Your care plan is with your care team for clinical review" (`carePlanInReview`), instead of "being prepared".
- **Every patient-side door is gated:** the portal care plan (`GET /api/gfc/care-plan`), the client/POA co-signature (refused `CARE_PLAN_AWAITING_CLINICIAN` before anything is written, since a patient cannot sign what they cannot see), the signed PDF (serves the visible version), the patient's documents list (only active versions), opening a version by id (a pending version answers "no such version", not "forbidden"), and the health panel's problem list taken from the plan.
- **Not changed:** caregivers still see the current plan's tasks, schedule and escalation even while it waits on a clinician. The ask named the patient; say if caregivers should follow the same rule.
- **Verified:** `test/care_plan_patient_visibility.test.js` (9 tests); 8 of 8 deliberate breaks caught (one survived first because the portal test matched an unrelated line, and was tightened). Full suite 2464 pass, 0 fail. `scripts/verify_care_plan_patient_visibility.js` 16/16 signed in as a real patient: an LMSW-written plan hidden and unsignable, visible once an RN co-signs, then v2 waiting while the patient still sees and downloads v1.
