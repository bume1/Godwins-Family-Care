# Signed tax forms show in Documents, and are never missing name or SSN

Owner-directed, 2026-11-14.

- **Documents list.** Every official form a person signs in Pay Setup (W-4, G-4, another state's certificate, I-9, W-9, W-8BEN) is listed under "What you signed" in My GFC → Documents. Open shows the filled government form. On the Staff Record in User Management the forms are listed for the **Super Admin only**, because they carry the full SSN; managers and coordinators still see the rest of the record.
- **Name, address and SSN first.** Pay Setup now lists "Legal name and mailing address" first, then the SSN, then the forms. A W-4, G-4 or state certificate cannot be signed until both are saved. The I-9 needs the name and address; its SSN box is optional by law. The SSN is required only once the payroll key is set, so it can actually be stored.
- **Corrections re-file.** Saving a corrected name, address or SSN files a fresh copy of each form already signed to their Drive folder. The Drive copy then matches what Open shows.
