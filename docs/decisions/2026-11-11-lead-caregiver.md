# A lead caregiver sees what was shared with the patient

Owner, 2026-11-11: a GFC caregiver on a client's care team is managing that client's health and portal, but is not a POA. "How do we give her access to view information sent or shared to the patient's chart." Owner choices: shared documents, medicines and allergies, visit summaries and appointments; only for caregivers an admin names, not everyone on the care team.

- **Not a portal login and not POA.** She is GFC workforce, so access comes through the caregiver app under a role-based "need to know" rule. POA stays a legal personal representative; a caregiver must never sign for the patient.
- **`careTeam.leadCaregivers`** on the client: an admin ticks "Lead caregiver" under the Primary or Backup Caregiver on the client's Care Team (`public/client-account-ui.js`). Changing who the primary or backup is drops anyone no longer on the team from the leads. `caregiverRepository.isLeadCaregiver` needs BOTH the tick and an assignment to the client; the tick alone grants nothing.
- **`GET /api/caregiver/clients/:clientId/shared`** (lead only, logged as `caregiver_shared_record_read`) serves, from the same published copies the patient's portal reads: the documents the patient can see (`clientDocuments.patientCanSee`, so an office document not shared with the patient stays hidden), medicines and allergies (published, else what the app holds, labelled by source), the patient-facing visit summaries and follow-up, and upcoming appointments. Documents open through `…/shared/documents/:docId/file`, with the same 404 rule as the patient's own download.
- **Never served to a caregiver:** the clinician's note, results, diagnoses, tests ordered, the problem list, vitals, billing or provider-to-family messages. Build-enforced in `test/lead_caregiver.test.js`.
- The caregiver app's client screen shows a **"Shared with <first name>"** card only when the server says `leadAccess: true`.
- Verified: `scripts/verify_lead_caregiver.js` 19/19 through the real server (removing the lead check fails 3); `test/lead_caregiver.test.js` (the assignment requirement mutation-checked directly, since the route already checks assignment first); rendered in Chromium; full suite green.
