# Make or paste a video room from My Day

Date: 2026-11-18 · Owner report (no paste box after the rebuild)

- My Day's visit details now offer **Create Video Room** and a **paste a Meet link** box on a video visit that has no room. Before this, you could only reach them from inside a started note.
- My Day fills in the visit notes (`pc_hometext`) for that day's rows before it summarizes them. The calendar list leaves those notes out, so a video visit's place read as null and the screen had no way to tell it was a video visit.
- Start Video Visit is still keyed on the room, never on the place.
