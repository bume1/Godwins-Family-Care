# Care Coordinator role (owner-directed, 2026-10-14)

"They need to be able to do everything in the chart except sign officially, prescribe, send orders… author notes and do all a clinician can for encounters / notes (scheduling included) but it be saved as a co-signer while the licensed provider signs off." And: "add the care coordinator role to the client's record so they show as a care team member."

- **What it is:** a new clinical role, `coordinator` (label "Care Coordinator"). In the admin hub it is a Role option that sets `role: caseManager` + `clinicalRole: coordinator`. It is **not licensed**: `hasClinicalAccess` stays false and `canClinicalWrite` is false.
- **Can do:** read the chart; start, save, edit and void a note draft (vitals, nursing and behavioral notes, screenings, home-visit notes, telehealth checks); book, move, cancel and no-show visits on any clinician's calendar; visit timing and video start; attach a note to a visit; file a scanned document into the client's file.
- **Cannot do:** sign or co-sign a note (`SIGN_COORDINATOR`), the clinician addendum, prescriptions, orders and referrals, coding, medication reconciliation, problem list and allergies, care plans, results, risk assessments, standing orders, publishing to the portal, AI review. Psychotherapy notes are refused (`PSYCHOTHERAPY_NOT_COORDINATOR`).
- **Default-deny, one new gate.** `requireClinicalDocumenter` (licensed clinician or coordinator) is on the note and calendar routes only. Everything else stays on `requireClinicalWrite`, so a route added later is closed to coordinators until someone opens it on purpose. A read-only case manager is still refused by both.
- **"Co-signer", as built:** the coordinator is recorded as the note's **preparer**, not a signer. Their saves are in the note history as always, and the signature block (OpenEMR text, in-app block, PDF) adds "Prepared by <name>, Care Coordinator (documentation support, not a signature)". The licensed clinician signs as normal and that signature is the only one that bills. A coordinator's name never appears as a signature, because an unlicensed signature on a clinical note would misstate who attested it.
- **Encounter provider:** a coordinator has no OpenEMR provider id, so a note started from a booked visit files under that visit's clinician (`pc_aid`). A note started without a visit files under the practice default with a warning; attaching it to the visit moves it to that clinician.
- **Screens:** the clinical workspace reads a new `access.canDocument` for Start visit, the note editor and the calendar. A coordinator sees a "Care Coordinator" chip and, in place of Sign & Submit, a card saying the clinician on the visit signs. `noteActions.canSign` is answered by the server.
- **Care team:** `careTeam.careCoordinators` (a list) on the client record, a "Care Coordinators" picker in the admin hub, shown on the portal care team as "Care coordinator". They share the case manager's messaging scope for that client and count as on the care team for referrals. Coordinators are left out of the Assigned Case Manager picker.
- **Bug closed on the way:** the boot migration that turns read-only case managers into LMSW ran every boot and would have overruled an admin who deliberately set a case manager to Read-only. Setting a clinical role on a case manager now stamps the migration marker, so the admin's choice holds. A care plan signature also refused unlicensed roles too loosely (an unknown role could fall through); it now requires a licensed role.
- **Verified:** `test/care_coordinator.test.js` (10 tests), 11 of 11 mutations caught (one survived first and the test was tightened). Nine existing guards repointed at the new gate names, none loosened. Full suite 2452, 0 failing. Both pages compile. Live over HTTP: a coordinator gets `canDocument: true`, `canWrite: false`, passes the note and appointment gates, and gets 403 on sign and prescriptions.
- **Not proven here:** a real OpenEMR encounter filed under the visit's clinician. **For the owner:** a coordinator needs their own OpenEMR account (no provider flag needed) and MFA, like every clinical workspace user.
