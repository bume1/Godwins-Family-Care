Godwins Family Care is a physician and FNP-led healthcare organization providing private home care, in-home clinical practice, and independent medical evaluations in Atlanta. We have two distinct paths of in-home care, plus a complementary medical service line through our nurse practitioners and collaborating physician.

Whether you need a companion for a few hours a week, full personal care for a loved one aging in place, or skilled nursing for complex medical needs, we have a service level that matches. Care coordination runs underneath every service we deliver, at every level. It's how we keep your whole care team informed and your needs ahead of the curve.

Foundation · Included in every service line

Care Coordination

Direct communication with your physicians, specialists, therapists, and care team. Documentation shared with families and providers. Proactive escalation when something changes. This is not an add-on or upsell — it is the brand promise that comes with being clinician-led, and it is included in every service across both tracks at every level.

Two-track service architecture

A sliding scale, not rigid tiers.

Track A covers non-medical home care under personal care and companion scope. Track B covers clinical home care delivered by skilled nurses and our nurse practitioner team. You enter at the level that fits today and move within or between tracks as needs change.

A
Track A · Non-Medical Home Care

Personal Care & Companion Services

Hands-on personal care, daily living support, and companionship delivered by trained caregivers in your home or facility.

A1
Companion & Wellness Visits
What's Included
Companionship, social engagement, supervised activity, transportation to appointments and outings, light errands, consistent presence between family visits.
I want consistent presence and engagement at home, but I don't need help with personal care yet. I might be lonely between family visits, recently moved into independent living, or recovering from something where I just need a reliable person checking in.
A2
Essential ADL Support
What's Included
Bathing, dressing, grooming, toileting assistance, transfers and mobility, feeding support, hygiene and incontinence care.
I need help with the physical tasks of daily living. Getting dressed, bathing safely, moving around the house. I'm still managing my home and meals, but my body needs hands-on support.
A3
Comprehensive Home Care
What's Included
Everything in A2, plus meal preparation, light housekeeping, medication reminders, transportation, companionship.
I need help with daily living and keeping the household running. Cooking, cleaning, getting to appointments, remembering medications. I want someone who can take the load off so I can focus on being well.
A4
Behavioral Support & Cognitive Wellness
What's Included
Everything in A3, plus dementia and Alzheimer's care, behavioral observation, structured routines, cognitive activities, crisis de-escalation, safety planning.
I'm managing memory loss, behavioral changes, or recent psychiatric needs. My family worries about my safety, agitation, or wandering. I need someone trained who knows how to keep me calm and safe without making me feel managed.
B
Track B · Clinical Home Care

Skilled Nursing & Provider Services

Skilled nursing visits, continuous shift nursing, and family nurse practitioner-led provider visits in your home or facility.

B1
Skilled Nursing Visits
What's Included
Wound care, medication administration including injections, vital sign monitoring with intervention, diabetic management, catheter and ostomy care, G-tube feeds, post-surgical observation. LPN with RN supervisory oversight. Visit length typically 1–2 hours.
I need specific clinical tasks done at home a few times a week. Wound care, injections, catheter care, blood sugar checks. I don't need constant nursing presence, just skilled hands for the tasks my family or I can't safely do.
B2
Continuous Skilled Care
What's Included
Extended LPN shift nursing in the home or facility. Four to twelve-hour shifts, up to 24-hour coverage for highest acuity. Continuous skilled monitoring, complex chronic disease management, post-discharge stabilization.
I need a nurse here for extended hours. Post-discharge recovery, advanced chronic disease, ventilator or trach care, or my family situation requires a nurse present rather than visiting.
B3
Provider Care Visits
What's Included
In-home assessments by your nurse practitioner, prescribing in coordination with your primary care provider, behavioral health medication management, chronic disease management visits, post-hospital follow-up, and care plan oversight.
I need a primary care or specialist provider who comes to me, especially for behavioral health med management, chronic disease oversight, or post-hospital follow-up. I want continuity between my home care team and my prescribing clinician under one roof.
What's Included vs. What You Can Add

Care Coordination & Case Management.

Care coordination is the foundation included in every Godwins service. Case management is professional psychosocial advocacy and care planning delivered by our LMSW team, available at two intensity levels. The two work together.

Included · Foundation

Care Coordination

Logistics across the existing care team. What our caregivers, LPNs, FNPs, and MDs do as part of delivering care, documented and communicated proactively to family and providers.
Examples In Practice
  • Caregiver notices Mom isn't eating well at meals. We document it, alert the PCP, follow up on whether labs or a med review are needed.
  • Daughter texts that Dad fell overnight. We alert the FNP, update the care plan with fall precautions, loop in PT if applicable.
  • Specialist's office calls about a missed appointment. We reschedule, coordinate transportation, notify the family.
  • Pharmacy flags a refill conflict on a new prescription. We loop the prescriber and document the resolution.
No additional cost. Part of every service we provide, at every level.
Add-On · Standard

Standard Case Management

Active clinical advocacy and planning for clients managing multiple providers. Delivered by our LMSW team, on a monthly care coverage. Stacks with any home care service.
Examples In Practice
  • Comprehensive psychosocial assessment at intake covering emotional wellbeing, cognitive function, family dynamics, and social support systems.
  • Master care plan integrating the PCP's chronic disease orders, the neurologist's recommendations, and the psychiatrist's regimen so all providers work from one document.
  • Supportive emotional check-ins for clients adjusting to a new dementia diagnosis or significant life changes.
  • Long-term care insurance advocacy when a benefit claim is denied. Documentation gathered, physician statements coordinated, appeal submitted.
Billed as a monthly care coverage. Professional services under our LMSW team.
Add-On · Intensive

Intensive Case Management

High-touch clinical management for clients in active transition or crisis. Includes weekly contact, real-time provider coordination, and active intervention authority.
Examples In Practice
  • Coordinating discharge from psychiatric hospitalization. Medication reconciliation, securing the seven-day post-discharge psychiatry appointment, communicating safety plan.
  • Convening a family meeting after a new dementia diagnosis. Decision-making roles set, legal and financial gaps surfaced, six-month care trajectory built collaboratively.
  • Navigating a Medicaid waiver application (Katie Beckett, ICWP). Medical documentation gathered, functional assessments completed, follow-up through approval.
  • Real-time provider coordination during complex transitions, including discharges from ICU to step-down to home.
Billed as a higher monthly care coverage. Weekly contact and active intervention authority.
Add-On Program · Pairs with any service

Continuous Care Program.

A hybrid program for memory care and complex chronic disease patients. Combines private-pay enhanced monitoring under the agency with Medicare-billable chronic care management under our nurse practitioner team's clinical practice.

Camera-based behavioral observation between caregiver visits, scheduled video check-ins, and a real-time family communication portal. Designed to extend clinical eyes beyond scheduled hours.

  • Blink camera setup and behavioral monitoring
  • Three video check-ins per defined window
  • Family portal for daily updates and clinical notes
  • Escalation pathway when monitoring surfaces concerns
Pricing details available on request.
Phase 2 · Coming Soon

Chronic Care Management

Under our FNP team · Billed to Medicare Part B

For clients with two or more chronic conditions, chronic care management provides Medicare-covered care coordination time delivered by clinical staff under FNP supervision within our practice.

  • Medicare-covered care coordination, billed to Part B and most Medicare Advantage plans
  • Twenty or more minutes monthly of dedicated coordination time
  • Comprehensive care plan signed by your nurse practitioner
  • Twenty-four-seven access to the care team for urgent needs
Standard cost share applies at your plan rate.
Phase 3 · Future Rollout

Remote Patient Monitoring

Under our FNP team · Billed to Medicare Part B

Adds Medicare-covered physiological monitoring with FDA-approved devices for blood pressure, weight, glucose, pulse oximetry, or heart rate. Layers on top of chronic care management, not a replacement.

  • Medicare-covered remote monitoring through approved devices
  • Daily readings reviewed by your care team
  • Early intervention when readings trend out of range
  • Coordinated with your existing chronic care management plan
Timing tied to device platform selection and Medicare enrollment.
Why this matters for you Phase 1 is available now under private pay. Phase 2 brings Medicare coverage for chronic care management as soon as Medicare enrollment for our clinical practice completes, layered on top of the same monitoring. Phase 3 adds remote patient monitoring once devices are in place. Across all three phases, you experience one coordinated care team while we handle the billing complexity behind the scenes.

How payment works

Clear, by service line.

Personal Care & Skilled Nursing

Personal care, companion services, skilled nursing visits, and continuous shift nursing are billed by Godwins directly. Paid through private pay, long-term care insurance (Genworth, Mutual of Omaha, Northwestern Mutual, John Hancock, Transamerica paneling in progress), VA contracts, and Georgia Medicaid waivers (Katie Beckett, ICWP) once Medicaid certification is approved.

Provider Care Visits

In-home visits with your nurse practitioner are billed as professional services through Medicare Part B, commercial insurance once credentialing completes, or private pay and membership at your preference. Billed separately from your home care invoice.

Labs, Imaging & Prescriptions

When a provider visit results in a need for bloodwork, imaging, or new medications, your nurse practitioner places those orders directly with the lab, imaging center, or pharmacy of your choice. Those facilities bill your insurance through their standard channels. Godwins does not process these claims. Standard insurance copays and deductibles apply at the ancillary facility.

Working Alongside Your Other Providers

If you currently receive Medicare home health benefits — intermittent skilled nursing combined with physical therapy, occupational therapy, speech therapy, or medical social services — those services are delivered by Medicare-certified home health agencies. We are not a Medicare home health agency, and we coordinate with the agency providing those services on your behalf.

When your Medicare home health benefit ends but you still need ongoing skilled care, we can pick up where the home health agency leaves off through private pay, long-term care insurance, Medicaid waiver, or VA benefits. Many of our clients use us specifically for the continuous shift nursing that Medicare home health doesn't cover.
