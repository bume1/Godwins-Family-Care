§ I · Premise

Godwins Family Care is a physician and FNP-led healthcare organization providing private home care, in-home clinical practice, and independent medical evaluations in the metro Atlanta area. We deliver care across two distinct paths — non-medical home care and clinical home care — with a complementary medical service line through our nurse practitioners and collaborating physician.

Whether you need a companion for a few hours a week, full personal care for a loved one aging in place, or skilled nursing for complex medical needs, we have a service level that matches today and a path to adjust as needs change.

Care coordination runs underneath every service we deliver, at every level. It is not an upsell; it is the brand promise that comes with being clinician-led. Case management, when needed, layers on top — delivered by our LMSW team and available at two intensity levels.

§ II · Foundation

Care Coordination

Included in every service we provide, at every level, on both tracks.

  • What it is Direct communication with your physicians, specialists, therapists, and care team — built into how we deliver care, not billed as a separate service.
  • What you see Documentation shared with families and providers. Proactive escalation when something changes. Single point of contact across your care team.
  • What it isn't Not an add-on. Not an upsell. Not a substitute for clinical case management when more intensive advocacy is needed.
  • Why it matters Most home care complaints stem from communication gaps. We removed the gap by making coordination part of the foundation, not the menu.
III The Service Architecture

A sliding scale, not rigid tiers.

Track A is non-medical home care — personal care and companion scope, delivered by trained caregivers. Track B is clinical home care — skilled nursing and our nurse practitioner team. Both tracks share the same care coordination foundation. Move within or between tracks as needs change.

Personal Care & Companion Services

Hands-on personal care, daily living support, and companionship delivered by trained caregivers in the home or facility.

A1
Companion & Wellness VisitsCompanion scope · Hourly
Companionship, social engagement, supervised activity, transportation to appointments and outings, light errands, consistent presence between family visits.
I want consistent presence and engagement at home, but I don't need help with personal care yet. I'm still independent — I just shouldn't be alone all the time.

Bathing, dressing, grooming, toileting assistance, transfers and mobility, feeding support, hygiene and incontinence care.

Comprehensive Home CarePersonal care + Home support
Everything in A2, plus meal preparation, light housekeeping, medication reminders, transportation, companionship.

Behavioral Support & Cognitive WellnessMemory care · Trained
Everything in A3, plus dementia and Alzheimer's care, behavioral observation, structured routines, cognitive activities, crisis de-escalation, safety planning.
I'm managing memory loss or behavioral changes. My family worries about agitation or wandering. I need someone trained to keep me safe without making me feel managed.
B Track BClinical Home Care
Nursing & Provider Scope

Skilled Nursing & Provider Services

Skilled nursing visits, continuous shift nursing, and family nurse practitioner-led provider visits in your home or facility.

B1
Skilled Nursing VisitsLPN with RN oversight · 1–2 hr
Wound care, medication administration including injections, vital sign monitoring with intervention, diabetic management, catheter and ostomy care, G-tube feeds, post-surgical observation.
I need specific clinical tasks done at home a few times a week. Skilled hands for what my family or I can't safely do — without needing a nurse here all day.
B2
Continuous Skilled CareLPN shift · 4–24 hours
Extended LPN shift nursing in the home or facility. Four-to-twelve-hour shifts, up to 24-hour coverage for highest acuity. Continuous skilled monitoring, complex chronic disease management, post-discharge stabilization.
I need a nurse here for extended hours. Post-discharge recovery, advanced chronic disease, ventilator or trach care. My situation needs a nurse present, not visiting.
B3
Provider Care VisitsFNP · Medicare Part B
In-home assessments by your nurse practitioner, prescribing in coordination with your primary care provider, behavioral health medication management, chronic disease management, post-hospital follow-up, care plan oversight.
I need a primary care or specialist provider who comes to me — for behavioral health med management, chronic disease oversight, or post-hospital follow-up under one coordinated team.

V Continuous Care Program

A hybrid program for memory care & complex chronic disease.

Combines private-pay enhanced monitoring under the agency with Medicare-billable chronic care management under our nurse practitioner team's clinical practice. Pairs with any home care service.

Phase 1 Available now

Enhanced Monitoring & Family Communication

Delivered by Godwins · Private pay

Camera-based behavioral observation between caregiver visits, scheduled video check-ins, and a real-time family communication portal. Designed to extend clinical eyes beyond scheduled hours.

  • Blink camera setup and behavioral monitoring
  • Three video check-ins per defined window
  • Family portal for daily updates and clinical notes
  • Escalation pathway when monitoring surfaces concerns
Pricing details available on request.
Phase 2 Coming soon

Chronic Care Management

Under our FNP team · Medicare Part B

For clients with two or more chronic conditions, chronic care management provides Medicare-covered care coordination time delivered by clinical staff under FNP supervision within our practice.

  • Medicare-covered care coordination, billed to Part B and most Medicare Advantage plans
  • Twenty or more minutes monthly of dedicated coordination time
  • Comprehensive care plan signed by your nurse practitioner
  • Twenty-four-seven access to the care team for urgent needs
Standard cost share applies at your plan rate.
Phase 3 Future rollout

Remote Patient Monitoring

Under our FNP team · Medicare Part B

Adds Medicare-covered physiological monitoring with FDA-approved devices for blood pressure, weight, glucose, pulse oximetry, or heart rate. Layers on top of chronic care management — not a replacement.

  • Medicare-covered remote monitoring through approved devices
  • Daily readings reviewed by your care team
  • Early intervention when readings trend out of range
  • Coordinated with your existing chronic care management plan
Timing tied to device platform selection and Medicare enrollment.

IX How Payment Works

Clear, by service line.

We bill by the service line that delivered the care. Some services run through home care channels; others run through our clinical practice.

Personal Care & Skilled Nursing

Billed by Godwins
Personal care, companion services, skilled nursing visits, and continuous shift nursing are billed by Godwins directly. Paid through private pay, long-term care insurance (Genworth, Mutual of Omaha, Northwestern Mutual, John Hancock; Transamerica paneling in progress), VA contracts, and Georgia Medicaid waivers (Katie Beckett, ICWP) once Medicaid certification is approved.

Provider Care Visits

Billed by our FNP team
In-home visits with your nurse practitioner are billed as professional services through Medicare Part B, commercial insurance once credentialing completes, or private pay/membership at your preference.

Labs, Imaging & Prescriptions

Billed by ancillary facility
Bloodwork, imaging, and prescriptions are placed by your NP with the lab, imaging center, or pharmacy of your choice — those facilities bill insurance through their own channels. Godwins does not process these claims.
