Two-track service architecture

A sliding scale, not rigid tiers.

Track A is non-medical home care, delivered by trained caregivers under personal-care and companion scope. Track B is clinical home care delivered by skilled nurses and our nurse practitioner team. Every service includes care coordination at no extra cost.

A1 Companion & Wellness

Companion & Wellness Visits

Companionship, social engagement, supervised activity, transportation, light errands, and consistent presence between family visits.

Where do I fit? I want consistent presence and engagement, but I don't need help with personal care yet. Maybe lonely between family visits, recently moved into independent living.
Hourly visits No personal care
A2 Essential ADL

Essential ADL Support

Bathing, dressing, grooming, toileting assistance, transfers and mobility, feeding support, hygiene and incontinence care.

Where do I fit? I need help with the physical tasks of daily living. Getting dressed, bathing safely, moving around the house. My body needs hands-on support.
Personal care Hands-on
A3 Comprehensive

Comprehensive Home Care

Everything in A2, plus meal preparation, light housekeeping, medication reminders, transportation, and companionship.

Where do I fit? I need help with daily living and keeping the household running. Cooking, cleaning, getting to appointments, remembering medications.
Most popular Whole home
A4 Behavioral & Cognitive

Behavioral Support & Cognitive Wellness

Everything in A3, plus dementia and Alzheimer's care, behavioral observation, structured routines, cognitive activities, crisis de-escalation, safety planning.

Where do I fit? I'm managing memory loss or behavioral changes. My family worries about agitation or wandering. I need someone trained to keep me safe without making me feel managed.
Dementia trained Memory care
B1 Skilled Nursing

Skilled Nursing Visits

Wound care, medication administration, vital sign monitoring, diabetic management, catheter and ostomy care, G-tube feeds, post-surgical observation. LPN with RN supervision.

Where do I fit? I need specific clinical tasks done at home a few times a week. Wound care, injections, blood sugar checks. Skilled hands for what my family can't safely do.
LPN / RN 1–2 hr visits
B2 Continuous Skilled

Continuous Skilled Care

Extended LPN shift nursing in the home or facility. Four-to-twelve-hour shifts, up to 24-hour coverage for highest acuity. Continuous monitoring and complex chronic disease management.

Where do I fit? I need a nurse here for extended hours. Post-discharge recovery, advanced chronic disease, ventilator or trach care. My situation needs a nurse present, not visiting.
4–24 hr shifts High acuity
B3 Provider Care Visits

Provider Care Visits

In-home assessments by your nurse practitioner, prescribing in coordination with your PCP, behavioral health medication management, chronic disease management, post-hospital follow-up, and care plan oversight.

Where do I fit? I need a primary care or specialist provider who comes to me — for behavioral health med management, chronic disease oversight, or post-hospital follow-up under one roof.
FNP visits Medicare Part B Prescribing
Coordination & case management

What's included, what you can add.

Care coordination is the foundation included in every Godwins service. Case management is professional psychosocial advocacy and care planning delivered by our LMSW team, available at two intensity levels. Stack with any home care service.

Included · Foundation

Care Coordination

Logistics across the existing care team. What our caregivers, LPNs, FNPs, and MDs do as part of delivering care, documented and communicated proactively to family and providers.

In practice
  • Caregiver notices Mom isn't eating well — we document, alert the PCP, follow up on labs or med review.
  • Daughter texts that Dad fell overnight — we alert the FNP, update fall precautions, loop PT in.
  • Specialist's office calls about a missed appointment — we reschedule, coordinate transport, notify family.
  • Pharmacy flags a refill conflict — we loop the prescriber and document the resolution.
No additional cost. Part of every service we provide, at every level.
Add-On · Standard

Standard Case Management

Active clinical advocacy and planning for clients managing multiple providers. Delivered by our LMSW team, billed as monthly care coverage. Stacks with any home care service.

In practice
  • Comprehensive psychosocial assessment at intake covering wellbeing, cognition, family dynamics, social support.
  • Master care plan integrating PCP, neurologist, and psychiatrist orders into one document.
  • Supportive emotional check-ins for clients adjusting to a new dementia diagnosis.
  • Long-term care insurance advocacy when a benefit claim is denied — documentation, appeals.
Billed as monthly care coverage. Professional services under our LMSW team.
Add-On · Intensive

Intensive Case Management

High-touch clinical management for clients in active transition or crisis. Includes weekly contact, real-time provider coordination, and active intervention authority.

In practice
  • Coordinating discharge from psychiatric hospitalization. Med reconciliation, the seven-day post-discharge psychiatry appointment, safety plan.
  • Convening a family meeting after a new dementia diagnosis. Decision-making roles, legal and financial gaps, six-month trajectory.
  • Navigating a Medicaid waiver application (Katie Beckett, ICWP) through approval.
  • Real-time provider coordination during complex transitions — ICU to step-down to home.
Higher monthly care coverage. Weekly contact and active intervention authority.
Add-on program · pairs with any service

Continuous Care Program.

A hybrid program for memory care and complex chronic disease patients. Combines private-pay enhanced monitoring under the agency with Medicare-billable chronic care management under our nurse practitioner team's clinical practice.

Phase 1 · Available now

Enhanced Monitoring & Family Communication

Delivered by Godwins · Private pay

Camera-based behavioral observation between caregiver visits, scheduled video check-ins, and a real-time family communication portal. Designed to extend clinical eyes beyond scheduled hours — closes the gap between visits where families worry most.

  • Blink camera setup and behavioral monitoring in primary living areas
  • Three video check-ins per defined window with our nursing team
  • Family portal for daily updates, photos, and clinical notes
  • Escalation pathway when monitoring surfaces concerns — falls, missed meals, agitation
Phase 2 · Coming soon

Chronic Care Management

Under our FNP team · Billed to Medicare Part B

For clients with two or more chronic conditions, chronic care management provides Medicare-covered care coordination time delivered by clinical staff under FNP supervision within our practice. Standard cost share applies at your plan rate.

  • Medicare-covered care coordination, billed to Part B and most Medicare Advantage plans
  • Twenty or more minutes monthly of dedicated coordination time
  • Comprehensive care plan signed by your nurse practitioner
  • Twenty-four-seven access to the care team for urgent needs
Phase 3 · Future rollout

Remote Patient Monitoring

Under our FNP team · Billed to Medicare Part B

Adds Medicare-covered physiological monitoring with FDA-approved devices for blood pressure, weight, glucose, pulse oximetry, or heart rate. Layers on top of chronic care management — not a replacement. Timing tied to device platform selection and Medicare enrollment.

  • Medicare-covered remote monitoring through approved devices
  • Daily readings reviewed by your care team
  • Early intervention when readings trend out of range
  • Coordinated with your existing chronic care management plan

How payment works

Clear, by service line.

We bill by the service line that delivered the care. Some services run through home care channels (private pay, LTC, VA, Medicaid waivers); others run through our clinical practice on Medicare Part B. We keep the categories clean so you always know what's billed where.

Home Care Service Lines

Billed by Godwins Family Care
Personal Care & Skilled Nursing

Personal care, companion services, skilled nursing visits, and continuous shift nursing. Paid through private pay, long-term care insurance (Genworth, Mutual of Omaha, Northwestern Mutual, John Hancock; Transamerica paneling in progress), VA contracts, and Georgia Medicaid waivers (Katie Beckett, ICWP) once Medicaid certification is approved.

Clinical Practice Service Lines

Billed by our nurse practitioner team
Provider Care Visits

In-home visits with your nurse practitioner, billed as professional services through Medicare Part B, commercial insurance once credentialing completes, or private pay/membership at your preference.

Labs, Imaging & Prescriptions

Bloodwork, imaging, and prescriptions are placed by your NP with the lab, imaging center, or pharmacy of your choice — those facilities bill insurance through their own channels. Godwins does not process these claims.
